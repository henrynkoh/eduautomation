# EduAutomation Tutorial

Welcome to **EduAutomation**, your platform for personalized education and training! This tutorial will guide you through using the platform to explore courses, track progress, and create custom learning workflows.

## Getting Started
1. **Visit the Platform**:
   - Go to `http://localhost:3000` (or your deployed URL).
2. **Explore Courses**:
   - Click "Explore Courses" on the homepage to view available classes.
   - Example: "Docker Fundamentals" or "n8n Workflow Automation."

## For Learners

### 1. Finding the Right Course

1. **Browse the Course Catalog**:
   - Navigate to the `/courses` page.
   - Use filters to narrow down by level, duration, or topic.

2. **View Course Details**:
   - Click on a course card to see its full description.
   - Review the curriculum, modules, and instructor information.

3. **Enroll in a Course**:
   - Choose between free (limited access) or paid (full access) options.
   - Click "Enroll Now" to begin your learning journey.

### 2. Tracking Your Progress

1. **Access Your Dashboard**:
   - Navigate to "My Courses" after enrollment.
   - View your progress across all enrolled courses.

2. **Complete Modules**:
   - Work through each module in sequential order.
   - Mark lessons as complete to track your progress.

3. **Take Quizzes**:
   - Complete quizzes at the end of each module to test your knowledge.
   - Review your results and identify areas for improvement.

### 3. Personalizing Your Learning

1. **Set Your Preferences**:
   - Update your profile with learning preferences.
   - Indicate your skill level and areas of interest.

2. **Get AI-Generated Content**:
   - Request additional explanations or examples on complex topics.
   - Use the AI tutor to get personalized help when needed.

## For Instructors

### 1. Creating Workflows

1. **Access the Workflow Editor**:
   - Navigate to `/workflows` and click "Create New Workflow".
   - Start from scratch or use a template.

2. **Design Your Automation**:
   - Add nodes for different actions (e.g., content generation, notifications).
   - Connect nodes to create a logical flow.

3. **Test and Deploy**:
   - Run a test execution to verify your workflow.
   - Activate the workflow to run automatically.

### 2. Generating Course Content

1. **Use AI Content Generation**:
   - Create a content generation workflow.
   - Specify your course title, target audience, and key topics.
   - Let the AI generate module outlines, explanations, and exercises.

2. **Refine the Content**:
   - Review the AI-generated content.
   - Edit and enhance as needed to match your teaching style.

3. **Organize Into Modules**:
   - Structure the content into logical modules.
   - Add assessments and interactive elements.

### 3. Monitoring Student Progress

1. **Set Up Progress Tracking**:
   - Create a student progress workflow.
   - Configure notifications for low engagement or milestones.

2. **Review Analytics**:
   - Check completion rates and quiz scores.
   - Identify areas where students may be struggling.

3. **Intervene When Needed**:
   - Send personalized encouragement to struggling students.
   - Provide additional resources for challenging topics.

## Advanced Features

### Docker Integration

1. **Deploy Your Own Instance**:
   - Use the provided Docker configuration to set up your environment.
   - Customize the `docker-compose.yml` file as needed.

2. **Scale as Needed**:
   - Add more container instances for higher traffic.
   - Configure volume persistence for data storage.

## Tips for Success

- **Start Small**: Begin with a single course or simple workflow before expanding.
- **Iterate Often**: Gather feedback and continuously improve your content.
- **Leverage Automation**: Use workflows to reduce manual tasks.
- **Personalize Learning**: Take advantage of AI capabilities to tailor education.
